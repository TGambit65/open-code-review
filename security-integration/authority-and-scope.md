## Permission and input boundary

The full action-by-target register in `scope.json` distinguishes **ALLOWED**,
**ROOT_ONLY**, **PENDING**, **NOT_AUTHORIZED** and **NOT_APPLICABLE**. Empty
writer-local denied/uncertain arrays mean no such action was attempted in this
bounded work, not that historical holds were cleared. Uncertain execution must
be reconciled before retry. Silence and elapsed time are not consent; a retry
requires materially new target-relevant evidence of known nonexecution and
valid authority, with no alternate bypass.

Authorized now: bounded pinned public source and tree-metadata reads, exact
approved-binary byte identity, six inert artifacts, and separate-context source
review when assigned. Reserved: new provider/API calls, credential access,
accounts or spending; scanner/native product execution; hooks, CI/settings or
security activation; production scans; protected/private unrelated source;
upstream contributions; writer publication or review requests.

The future design separates approved original scanner inputs from mapped and
hash-bound sanitized model inputs. It requires read-only isolation, denied
egress, resource/output limits and no inherited credentials or agent sockets.
Those mechanisms are **not implemented in S00**. No raw target-code/Git fallback
is authorized. This authoring step consumes only its approved public source
context and its own intended-public artifacts; no private target input is
claimed to have been sanitized or reviewed.
